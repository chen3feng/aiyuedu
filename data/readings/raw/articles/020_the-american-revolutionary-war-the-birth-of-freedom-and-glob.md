# The American Revolutionary War: The Birth of Freedom and Global Repercussions

> 美国独立战争：自由的诞生与世界的震荡

The

American Revolutionary War

(1775–1783) was a historic conflict in which thirteen British colonies in North America fought for and won their independence from Great Britain, giving birth to a new nation: the United States of America.

The primary

causes

of the war boiled down to economic oppression and political rights. Following the expensive Seven Years' War, the British Parliament imposed heavy taxes on the American colonies—such as the Stamp Act and the Tea Act—without granting them representation in Parliament. Outraged colonists rallied around the famous slogan:

"No taxation without representation!"

Tensions escalated into protests like the

Boston Tea Party

in 1773, prompting harsh British crackdowns.

In 1775, fighting erupted at the Battles of Lexington and Concord. The following year, on July 4, 1776, the Second Continental Congress issued the historic

Declaration of Independence

, drafted by

Thomas Jefferson

. Led by Commander-in-Chief

George Washington

, the Continental Army faced harsh winters, severe supply shortages, and untrained troops. However, turning points like the Battle of Saratoga convinced France and Spain to enter the war as allies, providing critical naval and financial support. In 1781, combined American and French forces trapped the main British army at the

Siege of Yorktown

, forcing their surrender.

The war reached its official

outcome

with the signing of the

Treaty of Paris in 1783

, in which Great Britain recognized the independence and borders of the United States.

The

far-reaching impact

of the war reshaped world history. Domestically, it led to the drafting of the U.S. Constitution and the creation of a modern federal republic. Globally, its ideals of liberty, democracy, and popular sovereignty inspired the

French Revolution

in 1789 and independence movements across Latin America, proving that a colony could break free from a global empire.

)

B

（解析：文中指出殖民地人民抗议英国只征税却不给予议会代表权，因而喊出了“无代表不纳税”的经典口号）

A

（解析：文中提到萨拉托加大捷后，法国和西班牙作为盟友参战，为美国提供了至关重要的海军和资金支持）
