# The Mystery of the Four Seasons: Earth's Tilt and the Wheel of Time

> 四季的奥秘：地轴的倾斜与岁月的轮回

<!-- 来源：Gemini 对话（share.gemini.google/6bKWnzs2Ol8h） · 原文 2589 字符 · 汉字占比 8% -->

Have you ever wondered why the year is divided into four distinct seasons: spring, summer, autumn, and winter? The names of these seasons come from old Germanic and Latin roots, carrying ancient meanings related to nature and farming. For instance, "spring" originally meant the "springing" of plants from the ground. Interestingly, the English language has two different names for the third season:

autumn

is more commonly used in British English, coming from the Latin word

autumnus

, while

fall

is popular in American English, originating from the poetic phrase "the fall of the leaf" when trees shed their leaves.

What causes these wonderful changes in weather and temperature? Many people think it is because Earth gets closer to or farther from the sun during its yearly journey, but that is a common myth! The true reason is

Earth's axis

. As Earth

orbits

the sun, its axis is

tilted

at an angle of about 23.5 degrees. This means different parts of the planet receive direct or slanted sunlight at different times of the year.

Because of this tilt, there is a big difference between the

northern and southern hemispheres

. When the Northern Hemisphere tilts toward the sun, it experiences warm summer days, while the Southern Hemisphere tilts away and experiences cold winter. When Earth moves to the opposite side of its orbit, the seasons flip! This is why Christmas is celebrated in the snowy winter in the United States, but on a warm summer beach in Australia. Through a simple tilt in space, our planet creates a breathtaking cycle of life and renewal!
