# Master Xuanzang's Pilgrimage: The Real Legend and Differences from the Novel

> 玄奘取经：真实的西行传奇与《西游记》的差异

When people hear the name

Xuanzang

(玄奘), they often think of the sensitive monk protected by magical disciples in the famous novel

Journey to the West

. However, the real history behind Master Xuanzang is far more courageous, dramatic, and inspiring than any fictional tale!

Xuanzang was his

dharma name

(monk name), while his

lay name

(birth name) was

Chen Hui

(陈祎). Disappointed by incomplete and contradictory Buddhist translations in China, he made a brave decision: to travel to India himself to collect original Sanskrit scriptures. Because the Tang government was at war, traveling abroad was banned. Xuanzang actually

smuggled

himself out of China, embarking on his dangerous journey in secret as a fugitive!

The real Xuanzang was not a weak man saved by Monkey Kings; he was a tall, fearless scholar with an iron will. He crossed blistering deserts and icy mountains alone, nearly dying of thirst and escaping bandits multiple times. After reaching India, he spent many years studying at

Nalanda Temple

, the world's highest center of Buddhist learning. Xuanzang became a master of philosophy and won high praise in India after defeating top scholars in grand

theological debates

.

Xuanzang's

far-reaching legacy

profoundly shaped East Asian culture. He returned to China with hundreds of precious scriptures, spending the rest of his life translating millions of words with unmatched precision. His detailed travel record,

Great Tang Records on the Western Regions

, provided rare geographical and historical data that modern archaeologists used centuries later to rediscover ancient sites across India and Central Asia. He remains one of the greatest cultural bridges in human history!

一、 单项选择题 (Multiple Choice)

What was Master Xuanzang's lay name (birth name) before becoming a monk?

A. Tang Sanzang

B. Chen Hui

C. Sun Wukong

How did Xuanzang initially leave Tang Dynasty China for India?

A. He was sent as an official ambassador by the Emperor.

B. He bought an official airline ticket.

C. He smuggled himself out secretly because traveling abroad was banned.

二、 判断正误 (True or False)

Modern archaeologists have used Xuanzang's book,

Great Tang Records on the Western Regions

, to locate and rediscover lost ancient sites in India and Central Asia. (

T / F

)

参考答案 (Answers)

B

（解析：文中提到了玄奘的俗名/本名是陈祎 Chen Hui）

C

（解析：文中指出当时唐朝禁止出境，玄奘是以“偷渡”的方式偷偷离开国境的）

T

（解析：文中提到玄奘撰写的《大唐西域记》为后世考古学家重新发现印度和中亚的古迹提供了极具价值的历史地理依据）
