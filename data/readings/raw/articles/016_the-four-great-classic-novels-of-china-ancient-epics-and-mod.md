# The Four Great Classic Novels of China: Ancient Epics and Modern Legends

> 中国四大古典名著：流传千古的东方史诗与现代传奇

<!-- 来源：Gemini 对话（share.gemini.google/6bKWnzs2Ol8h） · 原文 3227 字符 · 汉字占比 7% -->

China's "Four Great Classic Novels" are the most celebrated masterpieces in Chinese literature. Written over hundreds of years, they offer a deep window into Chinese history, philosophy, and society.

Here is the four-novel collection arranged by the

estimated time of writing

:

Romance of the Three Kingdoms

(Written in the 14th century, early Ming Dynasty)

Estimated Author:

Attributed to

Luo Guanzhong

(罗贯中).

Main Content:

Set during the turbulent fall of the Han Dynasty, this grand historical epic chronicles the power struggles, brilliant military strategies, and heroics among three warring kingdoms: Wei, Shu, and Wu.

Water Margin

(Written in the 14th century, early-to-mid Ming Dynasty)

Estimated Author:

Attributed to

Shi Nai'an

(施耐庵).

Main Content:

Set in the Song Dynasty, the story follows a band of 108 outlaws who gather at Mount Liang (Liangshan Marsh) to rebel against corrupt government officials and fight for justice.

Journey to the West

(Written in the 16th century, late Ming Dynasty)

Estimated Author:

Attributed to

Wu Chengen

(吴承恩).

Main Content:

A thrilling mythological fantasy following the Buddhist monk

Tang Sanzang

and his three magical disciples—including the legendary

Sun Wukong

(the Monkey King)—on an epic quest to retrieve sacred scriptures from India.

Dream of the Red Chamber

(Written in the 18th century, middle Qing Dynasty)

Estimated Author:

Written by

Cao Xueqin

(曹雪芹).

Main Content:

A deeply moving masterpiece centered on the romantic tragedy between

Jia Baoyu

and

Lin Daiyu

, recording the rise and tragic fall of a wealthy noble family in feudal China.

Today, these four masterpieces enjoy massive

modern influence

. Classic TV shows adapted from them have been rewatched billions of times across Asia. Furthermore, they are a endless source of inspiration for video games worldwide—from tactical games like

Total War: Three Kingdoms

to action blockbusters like

Black Myth: Wukong

, bringing these ancient tales to life for a global audience!
