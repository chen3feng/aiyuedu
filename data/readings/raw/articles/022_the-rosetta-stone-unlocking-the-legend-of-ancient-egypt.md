# The Rosetta Stone: Unlocking the Legend of Ancient Egypt

> 罗塞塔石碑：解锁古埃及文明的破译传奇

<!-- 来源：Gemini 对话（share.gemini.google/6bKWnzs2Ol8h） · 原文 3170 字符 · 汉字占比 8% -->

For over a thousand years, the mysterious picture-writing of ancient Egypt—known as

hieroglyphics

—remained a complete riddle to the world. Nobody living could read the secrets carved into tomb walls or papyrus scrolls. That all changed with the extraordinary discovery and decoding of a single slab of dark granodiorite: the

Rosetta Stone

.

In 1799, during Napoleon Bonaparte's military campaign in Egypt, French soldiers building a fort near the town of Rashid (Rosetta) uncovered a massive broken stone. The stone was unique because it featured the exact same decree—issued in 196 BCE by King Ptolemy V—carved in

three distinct scripts

:

Hieroglyphics

: The sacred script used for official and religious documents.

Demotic

: The everyday cursive script used by ordinary Egyptian people.

Ancient Greek

: The official language of the ruling Greek administration in Egypt.

Because scholars could still read Ancient Greek, they realized the stone provided a direct translation key! The race to decipher the Egyptian scripts began, becoming a battle of intellects between English polymath

Thomas Young

and French linguist

Jean-François Champollion

. Young made a crucial breakthrough by realizing that the oval rings—called

cartouches

—contained the phonetic spellings of royal names like "Ptolemy."

In 1822, Champollion achieved the ultimate breakthrough. He proved that hieroglyphics were not merely symbolic picture-puzzles, but a complex combination of

phonetic sounds

(letters/syllables) and

logograms

(words/ideas). By cracking the code, Champollion reopened the door to three thousand years of Egyptian history, allowing modern historians to finally read ancient texts directly! Today, the Rosetta Stone remains the centerpiece of the British Museum and a universal symbol of translation and discovery.

The oval rings found in hieroglyphic inscriptions, called cartouches, were used to highlight the phonetic names of royal figures. (

)

B

（解析：文中指出罗塞塔石碑刻有同一份诏书的三种不同文字，包括学者能够读懂的古希腊文，因而成为了破译的钥匙）

A

（解析：文中提到了法国语言学家商博良 Champollion 于1822年证明了古埃及象形文字是表音符号与表意符号结合的复杂体系，成功完成了破译）
