# The History of Rockets: From Ancient Fireworks to Iron Giants Reaching for the Stars

> 火箭发展史：从古老烟花到飞向星辰的钢铁巨兽

<!-- 来源：Gemini 对话（share.gemini.google/6bKWnzs2Ol8h） · 原文 3148 字符 · 汉字占比 8% -->

Have you ever looked up at the night sky and wondered how humans travel into outer space? The story of rockets is an epic adventure that spans hundreds of years, transforming simple bamboo tubes into towering spacecraft that can carry humans to the moon and beyond.

The history of rockets began in

ancient China

, where inventors mixed sulfur, charcoal, and saltpeter to create gunpowder. During the Song Dynasty, people attached gunpowder tubes to arrows to create "fire arrows" for celebrations and military defense. These were the earliest ancestors of modern rockets!

The leap into

modern rockets

happened in the 20th century, driven by visionary scientists.

Hermann Oberth

and

Robert Goddard

laid the theoretical and practical foundations for spaceflight, while

Wernher von Braun

later designed the legendary Saturn V rocket that took humans to the moon.

Engineers soon developed two main types of rocket propulsion:

liquid-propellant rockets

(which mix liquid fuel and liquid oxygen for high efficiency and precise control) and

solid-propellant rockets

(which are simple, stable, and ready to launch instantly, making them popular for military missiles and rocket boosters).

Today, rockets have grown to astonishing scales. The

Starship

developed by SpaceX is currently the

largest and most powerful rocket

ever built, standing taller than a 30-story building and designed to carry humans to Mars.

To make space travel cheaper and more sustainable,

new rocket technologies

are changing the game. Engineers are building advanced

full-flow staged combustion cycle engines

, testing eco-friendly and high-performance

methane fuels

, and pioneering

rocket reusability

—landing giant boosters vertically back on Earth so they can fly again and again!
