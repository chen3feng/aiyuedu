# Mars Exploration: Looking for a Second Home

> 火星探测：寻找人类的“第二家园”

<!-- 来源：Gemini 对话（share.gemini.google/6bKWnzs2Ol8h） · 原文 2425 字符 · 汉字占比 8% -->

Mars is often called the "Red Planet" because its soil contains a lot of iron.

For a long time, scientists have been asking a big question: Is there life on Mars?

Collider

To find the answer, humans have sent many spacecraft to explore this mysterious world. The United States (USA) has played a leading role in this space race. NASA has sent famous rovers like

Curiosity

and

Perseverance

to walk on the Martian rocks, take clear photos, and look for water.

Other countries are also joining the adventure. China made history with its

Tianwen-1

mission, successfully landing the

Zhurong

rover on Mars. The European Space Agency (ESA) and the United Arab Emirates (UAE) have also sent their own orbiters to study the planet's weather and atmosphere.

Mars exploration has also inspired great movies and TV shows!

In the famous movie

The Martian

, an astronaut gets left behind on Mars and has to grow potatoes to survive until a rescue team arrives.

Shows like

Mars

blend real science with exciting stories about building human cities on the planet.
