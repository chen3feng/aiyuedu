# The History of Firearms: From Black Powder Tubes to Modern Assault Rifles

> 枪械发展史：从黑火药竹筒到现代小口径突击步枪

The evolution of firearms spans nearly a thousand years, marking humanity's technological leap from primitive black powder weapons to modern high-precision, automatic firearms. This history reflects advances in metallurgy, chemistry, precision manufacturing, and military tactics.

1. 枪械的雏形与早期火绳时代 (Early Firearms)

突火枪 (Fire Lance / Tu Huo Qiang):

Invented in 13th-century Song Dynasty China, the

Tu Huo Qiang

is widely considered the earliest ancestor of firearms. Made of bamboo tubes packed with black powder and pellets (

Cipao

), it used ignited gunpowder to shoot projectiles over short distances.

火绳枪 (Matchlock Musket):

Developed in 15th-century Europe, the matchlock was the first shoulder-fired firearm with a mechanical trigger mechanism. A slow-burning match rope (slow match) was held by a serpentine mechanism and pulled down into a priming pan upon pressing the trigger. While a huge step forward, it performed poorly in wind and rain.

2. 燧发时代与定装弹药革新 (Flintlock & Breech-Loading)

燧发枪 (Flintlock Musket):

Invented in the early 17th century, the flintlock replaced the burning match rope with a piece of flint struck against a steel frizzen to produce sparks. It dominated global battlefields for over two centuries due to its reliability and weather resistance.

金属定装枪弹与后装枪 (Metallic Cartridge & Breech-Loading):

During the 19th century, two massive revolutions occurred:

Metallic Cartridges:

Integrated the primer, gunpowder, and bullet inside a single brass casing, making loading weather-tight, rapid, and uniform.

Breech-Loading (后装枪):

Replacing muzzle-loading (front loading), loading bullets into the rear (breech) of the barrel allowed soldiers to reload quickly while lying prone.

3. 无烟火药与栓动步枪黄金时代 (Smokeless Powder & Bolt-Action)

无烟火药 (Smokeless Powder):

Invented by Paul Vieille in 1884 (Poudre B), nitrocellulose-based smokeless powder burned far cleaner, produced dramatically higher gas pressures, and eliminated thick battlefield smoke screens. It enabled smaller caliber bullets to travel at unprecedented velocities.

栓动式步枪 (Bolt-Action Rifle):

Leveraging smokeless powder and internal magazines, bolt-action rifles became standard infantry weapons in WWI and WWII. Iconic rifles included the German

Gewehr 98 / Kar98k

, British

Lee-Enfield

, and Russian

Mosin-Nagant

.

4. 连发武器与速射革新 (Machine Guns & Submachine Guns)

加特林机枪 (Gatling Gun):

Invented by Richard Gatling during the American Civil War (1861), it featured multiple rotating barrels cranked by hand, capable of firing hundreds of rounds per minute.

机枪 (Maxim Machine Gun):

Hiram Maxim invented the first fully automatic machine gun (1884), utilizing recoil energy to automatically extract, eject, feed, and fire cartridges. It radically altered infantry defense tactics.

冲锋枪 (Submachine Gun / SMG):

Chambered in pistol-caliber rounds (e.g., 9mm Parabellum), SMGs like the German

MP40

and American

Thompson

provided devastating rapid fire at close ranges during WWII.

5. 半自动、自动步枪与小口径突击步枪 (Assault Rifles)

半自动步枪 (Semi-Automatic Rifle):

Fired one shot per trigger pull without manual bolt cycling (e.g., American

M1 Garand

).

突击步枪与小口径革命 (Assault Rifle & Small Caliber Era):

StG 44:

Germany created the world's first true assault rifle, firing an intermediate cartridge (

7.92×33mm Kurz

) that bridged full-power rifle rounds and pistol ammunition.

AK-47:

Designed by Mikhail Kalashnikov in 1947 (

7.62×39mm

), famed worldwide for its extreme durability, simplicity, and reliability.

M16 & Small Caliber Revolution:

Designed by Eugene Stoner (

5.56×45mm NATO

), the M16 pioneered small-caliber, high-velocity ammunition. Smaller rounds allowed soldiers to carry more ammunition while reducing recoil and improving control during full-automatic fire.

6. 侧枝演化：手枪的进化 (Pistols & Revolvers)

转轮手枪 (Revolver):

Perfected by Samuel Colt in the 1830s, featuring a revolving cylinder holding multiple chambers behind a single barrel, offering rapid multi-shot capability.

自动手枪 (Semi-Automatic Pistol):

Developed around the turn of the 20th century (e.g.,

Browning M1911

,

Luger P08

), using detachable spring-loaded box magazines inside the grip for faster reloading and higher round capacity.
