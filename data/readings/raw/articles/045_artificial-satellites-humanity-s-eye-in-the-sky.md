Sputnik 1 (October 4, 1957):

The Soviet Union launched

Sputnik 1

into low Earth orbit. A metal sphere equipped with radio transmitters, its simple "beep-beep" signal marked the official start of the Space Age and triggered the US-Soviet Space Race.

Dongfanghong-1 (April 24, 1970):

China launched its first artificial satellite,

Dongfanghong-1

(东方红一号), aboard a Long March-1 rocket. This milestone made China the fifth nation capable of launching satellites independently. The satellite broadcast the melody of

The East Is Red

back to Earth via radio.

Kessler Syndrome:

Proposed by NASA scientist Donald Kessler, this scenario warns that a high density of objects in LEO could trigger a chain reaction of collisions, where each impact creates fresh debris—eventually rendering specific orbital planes entirely unusable for generations.
